# Privacy Policy for Meshy 3D Studio PRO

**Last Updated:** October 2026

Meshy 3D Studio PRO ("the Extension") is dedicated to protecting the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use the Extension.

## 1. Single Purpose
Meshy 3D Studio PRO is an extension designed to inspect, preview, and export 3D models (GLB) generated by the user on the Meshy.ai platform.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
- **No Personal Information:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
- **No Authentication Credentials:** We do not collect, intercept, or store Meshy passwords, API keys, or payment details.
- **No Analytics / Telemetry:** We do not include third-party tracking scripts, advertising trackers, or analytical telemetry.
- **No Remote Code:** All code is executed locally within the Chrome Extension bundle.

## 3. Permissions Usage
- **Downloads (`downloads`):** Used solely to save GLB 3D model files initiated directly by user interaction.
- **Storage (`storage`):** Used locally in the browser to store user preferences (such as HUD position and minimized state).
- **Host Permissions (`https://*.meshy.ai/*`):** Required to display the 3D inspection studio and detect models generated in your Meshy workspace.

## 4. Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or transfer any user data to third parties.
